Viewer quick help

  • Scroll slices: mouse wheel or arrow keys up/down. Window/level: left-drag (default tool) or presets in the toolbar.
  • Zoom: right-drag; pan: middle-drag. Reset: toolbar “Reset view” or press Space.
  • Change series: click a series thumbnail in the left panel to show it in the selected (outlined) viewport.
  • Compare series: Layout button (grid icon), click a viewport to select it, then click a thumbnail (or drag thumbnails into viewports). Reference lines / crosshairs and the DICOM tag browser are in the toolbar “More tools” menu.
  • Every image is the original 16-bit DICOM pixel data; nothing was resampled or compressed.
  • The bracketed labels in the viewer's study list (e.g. [Left knee]) were added for orientation; the DICOM files themselves are unmodified.

Viewer: OHIF Viewer (open source). Not a regulatory-cleared diagnostic workstation; for primary reading please use the DICOM download.
